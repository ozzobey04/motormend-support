Motor Mend 3D – Support

Motor Mend 3D is a calm 3D puzzle game where you repair vehicles by sorting screws into drawers by color.

Contact us

For questions, bug reports or suggestions: ozanaslanao@gmail.com. We usually reply within a few business days.

FAQ

Did I lose my progress? Progress is stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes your progress.

Are there in-app purchases? No. Coins are earned in-game only.

My daily gift did not arrive. The daily gift uses your device clock; make sure the time is set automatically.

I cannot tap a screw. Tap the camera-reset button at the top right, or enable numbered screw controls in Settings.
